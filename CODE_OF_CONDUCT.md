# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to
Blindbandit Records projects — regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity, experience level, nationality, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent
- Respect privacy and accessibility needs

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, trolling, hate speech, doxxing, or publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **business@mrblindbandit.net**.

## Scope

Applies to this repository’s issues/PRs and official Blindbandit project spaces.
